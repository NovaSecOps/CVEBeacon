Coverage unknown means the scan could not check that item, so its status is not known. Not affected means the recorded assessment found no match.

CVSS is a 0-10 severity score; higher numbers mean more severe flaws. CVE is a public ID for one known security flaw. CISA and EU KEV are lists of flaws known to be actively exploited. EPSS estimates the chance of exploitation in the next 30 days. Retained findings keep their original observation time. Removed assets and changed versions are labelled explicitly.

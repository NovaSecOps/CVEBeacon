Operations run as separate cvebeacon processes. Operations that write the same storage share locks. Manual jobs wait in the queue for scheduled or command-line runs to finish, for up to six hours from submission. Channel tests use a separate lane with one test at a time. Each job records its start and outcome in the account audit trail.
